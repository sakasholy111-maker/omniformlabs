1.Summary

JobWeek is a planner for shifts, pay, tips, bills, and everyday calculations. It does not require an account. We do not sell personal information, and we do not display advertising.

Shift names, times, hourly rates, tips, expenses, bills, and reminder choices stay on your device. JobWeek does not upload that information to Omniform Labs servers.

2.Shifts and Money on Device

Records are stored locally with Apple's on-device storage. They exist so the calendar, income list, expenses, and reports can function. Deleting the app removes that local store from the device.

3.Reminders

If you allow notifications, JobWeek can schedule a local reminder 60 minutes before a shift and another 10 minutes before. The notification can include the shift name and start time. It does not include amounts. Reminder text on the home card is chosen on device from a fixed library.

4.Subscriptions

JobWeek Premium is billed by Apple. The app uses Adapty to activate the SDK, read whether the premium access level is active, present the paywall, and restore purchases. Apple and Adapty process the identifiers needed for purchase and restore. Payment details stay with Apple. Omniform Labs does not receive your card number.

5.Tracking

JobWeek may ask for permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt so subscription performance can be measured. If you decline, the planner still works. JobWeek does not use tracking to share your data with third-party advertisers.

6.Support

If you email sakasholy111@gmail.com, the message contains only what you choose to send. Rate and Share hand the request to Apple or the system share sheet.

8.Children
