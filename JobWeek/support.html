4.Common Topics

The Home Line Did Not Change

Lines are chosen from a fixed library by the time of day. A new six-minute slice, a new hour, the weekend, or a shift that is about to start will select a different line. Opening the app twice in the same few minutes can show the same line on purpose, so the card does not flicker.

Plans or Restore

View Plans and Restore are in Settings. Purchases use the Apple ID on the device. If Restore says nothing was found, the subscription is not active on that Apple ID. The paywall itself is provided by Adapty and appears after launch and when a premium feature is opened.

A Shift Stays on the Calendar

Done marks the shift with a green line. Skip marks it with a gray line. Open it again to read the details. Past days that are still scheduled are not editable.

Notifications

Reminders are local. Allow notifications in iOS Settings if the 60-minute and 10-minute alerts do not appear. They include the shift name and time, not the pay.
