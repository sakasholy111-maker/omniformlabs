1.Summary

CueFlow is a teleprompter application that lets you create scripts, import readable text files, preview lyric-style captions, and start floating captions with Picture in Picture. When you tap Start or use voice dictation, CueFlow may use the microphone and speech recognition so captions can follow your English reading and so spoken words can be inserted into a script. CueFlow does not require account registration. We do not sell personal information, and we do not display advertising.

CueFlow is designed to keep your script workflow local. Your script text is stored on your device unless you choose to share it outside the app through iOS features or by contacting support. CueFlow does not upload your script text or microphone audio to Omniform Labs servers.

2.Scripts and Local Storage

Scripts you create or edit in CueFlow are stored locally on your device using Apple's app data storage technologies. Script titles, script body text, creation dates, update dates, and estimated reading duration may be saved so the script library can function.

CueFlow does not upload script text to Omniform Labs servers. We do not operate a user account system, sync service, or remote script database for CueFlow.

3.Imported Files

CueFlow can import readable text from user-selected TXT, PDF, DOCX, RTF, and ODT files, including UTF-16 plain text. Import only happens after you choose a file through the system file picker. The selected file is read locally so CueFlow can create a script from readable text.

If a PDF, DOCX, RTF, or ODT file does not contain readable text, CueFlow may be unable to create a script from it. CueFlow does not transmit imported files to Omniform Labs servers for extraction or processing.

4.Microphone and Speech Recognition

CueFlow requests Microphone and Speech Recognition permission when you tap Start for floating captions, or when you use voice input in the script editor. These permissions are optional. If you decline, you can still create, import, edit, and preview scripts. Voice-following captions and dictation will not run until you grant access.

When granted, CueFlow uses these permissions only to:

  • follow your English reading position and highlight the current words in the selected script;
  • convert spoken English into text that you can insert into a script in the editor.

Speech processing is performed through Apple speech APIs on your device. CueFlow does not record a lasting audio file of your session, does not store raw microphone audio in the script library, and does not upload microphone audio or speech transcripts to Omniform Labs servers. CueFlow does not use your voice to train advertising models or to identify you across other companies' apps.

You can change or withdraw these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. If access was previously denied, CueFlow may open Settings so you can enable Microphone and Speech Recognition, then return and tap Start again.

Not a recorder. CueFlow is not a camera, microphone, or screen-recording product. It does not capture other apps, and it does not publish audio or video. Any recording you make in Camera or another app is controlled by that app and by iOS.

5.Floating Captions

CueFlow uses Picture in Picture and local rendering to display floating captions. Caption frames are generated on your device from the selected script, theme, mirror setting, and voice-follow position.

Picture in Picture may use the system's audio session so the caption window can remain visible when you switch to another app. CueFlow does not play or publish media audio as a content service. Any video you create in another app is controlled by that app and by iOS.

6.Network Use

CueFlow's core script library, import, preview, dictation, voice follow, and floating caption features are designed to work without sending script content or microphone audio to us. Some system actions, such as opening a support email, opening legal pages, sharing an App Store link, checking subscription status with Adapty, restoring purchases, presenting Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, or using Apple services, may require a network connection handled by iOS or the destination service.

7.Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, device model, app version, iOS version, screenshots, and issue description. We use this information only to respond to the request and maintain support records.

Do not send private scripts, voice recordings, or confidential video plans in a support email unless you want us to review that content as part of your request.

8.Tracking and Advertising

CueFlow does not show advertising and does not sell your personal information. CueFlow may present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt so subscription performance can be measured and CueFlow can be improved. Your data is not shared with third parties for their advertising.

You can allow or deny tracking. Denying tracking does not block script creation, import, preview, or subscribed floating captions. Apple and Adapty may still process identifiers required to validate App Store purchases and restore subscription access.

Microphone audio is used only for voice follow and dictation through Apple speech APIs. CueFlow does not upload this audio to Omniform Labs servers.

10.Children's Privacy

CueFlow is intended as a general productivity and creator utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support request, contact us so we can delete it.

11.Security

Script data is stored inside the app's local container and protected by iOS device security features. You are responsible for protecting your device passcode, iCloud backups, and any screenshots or videos you create outside CueFlow.
